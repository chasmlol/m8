Mission: Two Betrayals

Story so far: On the ring world Halo, {player} left Cortana, the AI they carry, in the control room to find Captain Keyes, lost when the Flood, parasites that turn victims into monsters, overran his team. Spark admitted it and set his Sentinels on them to seize her. Cortana now plans to destroy Halo by blowing up the crashed Pillar of Autumn's reactors.
