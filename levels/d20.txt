Mission: Keyes

Story so far: Cortana, the AI {player} carries, learned that firing the ring world Halo would wipe out all sentient life in the galaxy to starve the parasitic Flood. Halo's Monitor, 343 Guilty Spark, wants it fired and is now their enemy. Cortana can destroy Halo by blowing up the crashed Pillar of Autumn's reactors, but needs Captain Keyes, missing since the Flood overran his team, or his neural implants. After {player} wrecked Halo's pulse generators, Cortana teleported them to Keyes.

This mission: They arrive in a crippled Covenant cruiser overrun by the Flood. A Covenant strike team is fighting them, afraid the Flood will fix the ship and escape Halo in it. Keyes is alive, his implants intact, and {player} must find him.
